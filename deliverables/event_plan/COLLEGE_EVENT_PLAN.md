## 1. Objective & Target Audience

### The Problem on Campus
Engineering students at Karpagam Institute of Technology graduate with strong technical capabilities in programming, web development, and cloud computing, but often lack direct commercial exposure to enterprise fintech infrastructure—such as corporate spend management, card issuing rails, automated vendor payments, and API banking.

### Event Goal
To conduct a full-day, multi-track fintech and entrepreneurship summit that connects 1,000+ students with regional fintech practitioners, alumni founders, and startup mentors, providing:
1. Practical understanding of B2B spend management and financial software architecture.
2. A platform for student engineering teams to pitch early-stage software and hardware venture ideas.
3. Concrete internships and career connections in modern financial technology.

### Target Headcount & Scale (Estimates)
- **Proposed Registrations**: 1,200 students (Open to KIT Coimbatore and sister institutions under Karpagam Educational Institutions).
- **Target Attendance**: 1,000 verified attendees (Capacity of Kalam Auditorium: 1,200 seats).
- **Shortlisted Student Pitch Teams**: 20 teams (4–5 students per team across 2nd, 3rd, and final year batches).

## 5. College Permissions & Institutional Approvals (Administrative Workflow)

To execute an event at Karpagam Institute of Technology, formal administrative clearance follows a four-tier approval hierarchy:

1. **Principal, Karpagam Institute of Technology**:
   - *Submission*: Detailed 6-page event brief outlining educational objectives, student attendance estimates, safety protocols, and zero-alcohol/non-political declaration.
   - *Required Sanction*: Official Campus Event Order authorizing auditorium usage and academic OD (On-Duty) attendance status.
2. **Karpagam Educational Institutions (KEI) Management & Accounts Section**:
   - *Submission*: Sponsorship MOUs and proposed budget.
   - *Required Sanction*: Approval to accept corporate sponsorship into the college club bank account and authorization for vendor bank disbursements.
3. **Head of Department (HoD) - Computer Science & Information Technology**:
   - *Submission*: Academic calendar cross-check ensuring no clash with Anna University internal assessments or laboratory exams.
   - *Required Sanction*: Student volunteer allocation and faculty advisor endorsement.
4. **Campus Estate & Security Office**:
   - *Submission*: Gate-pass clearance requests for external guest vehicles entering via L&T Bypass Road Eachanari Gate, and requisition for diesel generator (DG) backup for Kalam Auditorium.
   - *Required Sanction*: Security guard deployment for auditorium entrance crowd control.

## 7. Recording, Photography & Media Documentation Plan

### Recording Equipment & Setup
- **Camera 1 (Main Stage Wide)**: Fixed Sony 4K camera on center aisle tripod capturing full stage, banner, and audience scale.
- **Camera 2 (Podium & Speaker Tight)**: Fixed 4K camera on stage-left capturing speaker close-ups and screen presentations.
- **Camera 3 (Roving Gimbal Operator)**: Sony A7IV on DJI Ronin gimbal for audience reactions, student pitch demonstrations, and b-roll.

## 8. Event-Day Operations & Crisis Mitigation

### Team Role Assignments
- **Stage Manager (1 Student Lead)**: Controls run-of-show timing, speaker podium transitions, and digital countdown buzzers.
- **Registration Desk Crew (6 Students)**: QR code scanners, badge distribution, and guest registration.
- **Audio-Visual Crew (4 Students)**: Slide presentation laptop, soundboard monitoring, microphone battery replacements.
- **VIP Hospitality Leads (2 Students)**: Guest reception at Eachanari Gate, green-room refreshments, and speaker transport coordination.
- **Ushering & Crowd Control (10 Volunteers)**: Auditorium door management and aisle crowd flow.

### Anticipated Real-World Crises & Mitigation Protocols
1. **Audio / Microphone Failure**:
   - *Risk*: Wireless lapel mic drops RF frequency or batteries die during keynote.
   - *Mitigation*: A wired Shure SM58 microphone is permanently connected and tested on the center podium as a hot-standby, switchable within 15 seconds.
2. **Power Grid Interruption**:
   - *Risk*: TNEB power fluctuation in Seerapalayam / Eachanari area.
   - *Mitigation*: Advance requisition with Estate Office for dedicated 125 kVA generator backup running in parallel with automatic phase changeover.
3. **Slide / Laptop Resolution Mismatch**:
   - *Risk*: Visiting speaker's laptop fails to display via auditorium HDMI projector.
   - *Mitigation*: All pitch decks and keynote slides collected 24 hours prior, vetted, and pre-loaded onto a unified master presentation laptop with clicker.

## 9. Post-Event Review, Measurement & Lessons Learned

### Proposed Success Metrics
1. **Attendance Rate**: Goal: ≥80% attendance of registered students (Minimum 950 checked-in students).
2. **Student Startup Pipeline**: Goal: 20 completed student pitches evaluated by external judges, with top 3 teams receiving cash grants and incubation support from Karpagam IEDC.
3. **Budgetary Adherence**: Goal: 100% adherence to ₹3,20,000 budget with zero out-of-pocket deficit for students or the institution.
4. **Attendee Satisfaction**: Digital feedback survey administered at exit; Goal: ≥85% positive rating on speaker relevance and workshop practicality.

### Anticipated Lessons for Subsequent Editions
- **Pre-Registration Cap**: Cap registrations per engineering department to ensure balanced representation across non-CS branches (Mechanical, ECE).
- **Mobile Digital Badge Scanner**: Replace paper printed attendance check sheets with native barcode scanning on volunteers' smartphones to reduce morning lobby queuing.
- **Longer Networking Interval**: Expand afternoon networking high-tea from 30 to 45 minutes to give student founders more 1-on-1 time with visiting venture judges.
